## v5 changes
- **Light slides only:** the dark code slide now uses the template's Code Light layout, and the repo tree is light.
- **Week tracker** (Mon Tue Wed Thu Fri, current day in pink) at the top of every story slide, and each title starts with its day, so viewers always know where they are in the story.
- **Emojis, used sparingly:** 👩‍💼 the tech lead, 🧑‍💻 Sam, 🤖 Codex (avatars and chat labels), and ✅ on Friday's approval.
- **More animation:**
  - chat bubbles wipe in from their own side
  - checklist items and ticks wipe from the left
  - the "3 rounds" badge and "Approved" bubble pop in with a circle effect, then pulse
  - banners use a box reveal, and task cards drop in
  - every slide has a fade transition, and the statement and quotation slides use a wipe transition
